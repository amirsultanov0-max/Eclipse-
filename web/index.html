Eclipse

A small Transformer language model trained from scratch on TinyStories, a dataset of simple children's stories. Write the beginning of a story and Eclipse continues it.

Eclipse is a text-completion model, not an assistant. It does not answer questions or follow instructions, and it only knows the style of short stories for young children. Its continuations often drift, repeat, or contradict themselves.
